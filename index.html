The studio

Stories first

Intrecci di Carta (“paper weaves”) is the label under which novelist and game designer Pierpalmiro Stroppa publishes video games. Every game starts from a story and an idea that's easy to explain. Then it gets played on a phone, fixed and played again, until it's really fun.

Pier also writes novels and gamebooks: visit the books site →
