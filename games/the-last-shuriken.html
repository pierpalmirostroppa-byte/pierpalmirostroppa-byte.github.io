The Last Shuriken

One ninja, the night and an army of zombies. Every swipe throws a shuriken.

First-person action13+In development
In developmentTell me when it's out

The world has fallen. At night the zombies come out of the woods, and you are a ninja with pockets full of shuriken. Then a signal on the radio: somewhere, there are still survivors.

The Last Shuriken is a first-person action game: move the ninja with the stick and throw with your finger, one swipe for every shuriken. The game is in development: images come from test builds and may change.

01

One swipe, one shuriken

Like slicing fruit, but against zombies. On the right, buttons for a straight throw and a close-range hit.

02

11 weapons to find

Katana, chainsaw, exploding kunai, a bow with fire arrows, electric shuriken, a flamethrower, nunchaku and more. Pick them up and use them until they run out.

03

Woods, graveyard, hospital

And a supermarket full of surprises. Ever harder waves and a boss at the end of every third wave.
