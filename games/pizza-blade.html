Pizza Blade

Slice pizzas, cakes & bread in 3D! 4 worlds, 100 levels, real cuts, giant bosses

3D arcadeComing soon to Google Play
COMING SOON ONGoogle PlayBecome a tester
↓ Already a tester? Download the test version

Pizza Blade is pure arcade, and now it's all in 3D: food launches upward and you have one job, slice it mid-air with a sharp swipe. One clean cut, the pizza splits open. Points. Miss your target, though, and you lose a life.

Sounds easy. Then the pace picks up, and a 3D globe takes you on a journey through four worlds, each tastier than the last.

01

Four tasty worlds

A pizzeria in Italy, a pastry shop in Austria, a bakery in France and Christmas, with a reindeer dashing by, chased by Santa himself.

02

Real cuts from level 13

Every good food needs three strokes: wedges on pizzas and cakes, slices on bread. Two parallel strokes won't do.

03

100 levels, giant bosses

First speed, then armored food, up to bosses that cross the screen. Pizza Fever, golden pizzas and 5 blades to unlock.

04

The golden rule

Only slice the good food. Red ring? Hands off: pineapple pizza, moldy bread, the mouse. Green ring? Bonus, slice away!
