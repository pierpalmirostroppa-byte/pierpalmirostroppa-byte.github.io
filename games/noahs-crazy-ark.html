Noah's Crazy Ark

Carry the animals to the ark before the flood! Dodge dinos and beat monsters.

3D family adventurePEGI 3Coming soon to Google Play
COMING SOON ONGoogle PlayBecome a tester
↓ Already a tester? Download the test version

It's raining cats and dogs, the water is rising, and the animals won't board the ark by themselves. Help Noah carry them one at a time, tucked under his arm, before the flood swallows the plateau!

A funny 3D adventure for the whole family, with cartoon characters, five worlds to explore and a monster waiting at the end of each one.

01

18 species to rescue

The lizard leaves its tail in your hand, the piglet slips away if you sprint, the baby goat wriggles until you hop, and the skunk keeps the dangers away.

02

Run, jump, climb

Double jump, roll, vines, swimming and diving. Some animals live in the treetops and on rocky ledges: you'll have to go up and get them.

03

The weirdest dangers

Meteors, a T. rex that's hopeless at corners, a griffin that steals animals, the Chickodile, the Giraffecopter and the Accordion snake.

04

5 worlds, 5 monsters, no blood

Werewolf, Medusa, centaur, minotaur and cyclops. Beaten monsters run off crying, fall asleep or make peace.
